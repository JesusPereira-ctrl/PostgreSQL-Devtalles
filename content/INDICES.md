# Indices

Imagine a library where you need to find a specific book. If the library lacks categories or an efficient way to locate items, you will likely spend more time than necessary finding what you need. This is where indexes come in: they essentially alert the database to prepare for potential queries, ensuring that when you search for something, it can be retrieved quickly. However, keep in mind that this comes at a cost—it impacts both storage requirements and the performance of write operations.
